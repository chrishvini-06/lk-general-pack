## 0.1.0 (draft, not released)

First draft of the Sri Lanka general statutory pack, prepared with an AI assistant for review by the named author.

**Covers**
- VAT: standard rate 15% (1 Sep 2022 to 31 Dec 2023) and 18% (from 1 Jan 2024); zero-rated, exempt and out-of-scope codes; annual registration threshold.
- SSCL: 2.5% levy borne by the business, on 100% (services), 50% (wholesale or retail) or 85% (manufacture) of turnover, from 1 Oct 2022; exempt code; annual registration threshold reduced to LKR 36 million (LKR 9 million a quarter) from 1 Jul 2026.
- Withholding tax on service fees: 5% to resident individuals and 14% to non-residents, from 3 Jun 2026 (the date of Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 11 of 2026 and the regime in IRD Circular SEC/2026/E/04).

**Not covered** (see README and capability-requests.md): VAT on financial services, rent and interest withholding, dividends, imports, registered distributors, special schemes.

**Law cited:** VAT Act No. 14 of 2002 as amended, including Act No. 14 of 2026; Social Security Contribution Levy Act No. 25 of 2022 as amended by Acts No. 24 of 2025 and No. 10 of 2026; Inland Revenue Act No. 24 of 2017 as amended by Act No. 11 of 2026.

**Before and after values:** none (first release).
